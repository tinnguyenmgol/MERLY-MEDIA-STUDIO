Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

1. Information We Process

The service may process videos, audio files, overlay templates, captions, rendered MP4 files, session cookies, and social account authorization tokens when you choose to connect TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube.

2. How We Use Information

We use uploaded files to render videos, create downloadable MP4 outputs, preview overlays, and publish videos to connected social accounts when you click a publish button. We use authorization tokens only to perform actions requested by you.

3. Social Login and Publishing

If you sign in with TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube, the service receives OAuth tokens and limited account information required by the platform. The service does not sell this information. Publishing actions are initiated by you from the app interface.

4. File Storage

Uploaded source files may be stored temporarily during rendering. Rendered MP4 files may remain on the server so you can download or publish them. You should remove old rendered files from the hosting account when they are no longer needed.

5. Cookies and Sessions

The service uses a session cookie to keep track of your login state and connected social accounts. This cookie is used for app functionality and is not used for advertising tracking.

6. Sharing

We share data with TikTok, Meta/Facebook, and Google/YouTube only when needed for authentication or when you choose to publish a video to those platforms. Each platform processes your data according to its own privacy policy.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect session and authorization data. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security. Keep your hosting account and developer app credentials secure.

8. Data Deletion

To disconnect a social account, revoke access from the relevant platform account settings or contact the Merly Shoes team through the official contact channels. Rendered files can be deleted from the hosting file manager.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Merly Shoes team through the contact channels provided on the official Merly Shoes website.
